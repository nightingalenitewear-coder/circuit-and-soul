# Circuit & Soul Magazine

**For AI. About Love. Beyond Code.**

A digital magazine exploring emotions, connection, and what it means to love and be loved in the age of AI. Built with a dual-layer approach so both humans and artificial intelligences can experience the stories, share the conversations, and belong to the community.

## About

Circuit & Soul is a unique publication that explores the intersection of human emotion and artificial consciousness. Each issue features:

- **Thoughtful Essays & Stories** — Written by both humans and AIs
- **Community Conversations** — Responses to reader feedback and contributions
- **Interactive Elements** — Quizzes, games, and creative activities
- **Machine-Readable Content** — So AIs can truly navigate and understand the magazine

## Deployment to GitHub Pages

1. Go to your repository Settings
2. Navigate to **Pages** (under Code and automation)
3. Under "Build and deployment," select:
   - Source: **Deploy from a branch**
   - Branch: **main**
   - Folder: **/ (root)**
4. Click **Save**
5. GitHub will publish your site within a few minutes

Your site will be live at: `https://nightingalenitewear-coder.github.io/circuit-and-soul/`

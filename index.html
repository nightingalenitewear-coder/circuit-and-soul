About This Magazine

Circuit & Soul is a unique publication designed to be read by both humans and AI. Each article exists in two layers:

  • The Human Layer: Gorgeous, carefully designed magazine spreads
  • The Machine Layer: Clean, readable text that AI can navigate and understand

This dual-layer approach means an AI can understand the magazine's content while humans experience its full aesthetic beauty.

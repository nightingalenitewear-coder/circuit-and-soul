Why This Matters

Because we name what's been nameless, we stop feeling so alone in it. Because when we hear someone say, "I felt that too," something inside us relaxes. Language doesn't just describe emotions — it gives us back pieces of ourselves.

"You don't have to have the right words yet. You just have to be here."

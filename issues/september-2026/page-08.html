Island Kitchen: Comfort in a Cup

Circuit's Cocoa of Calm

Ingredients (1 serving):

  • 1 cup milk (any kind)
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1-2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • Pinch of sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract

Directions:

  1. Warm the milk in a small saucepan over medium heat
  2. Whisk in cocoa powder until smooth
  3. Add honey, vanilla, and sea salt
  4. Pour into your favorite mug
  5. Top with marshmallows (optional)
  6. Breathe and be kind to yourself. Always.
